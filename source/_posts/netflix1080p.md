1. 用Chrome打开Netflix，搜索test，播放TEST PATTERNS

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-39899319bebecce98241c6370d91d094_r.jpg)

2. 右上显示1280×720，即影片画质被限制到了720P

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-33f1e6172c6775eaf3aeee7306ab653a_r.jpg)

3. 打开 https://github.com/truedread/netflix-1080p ，点击Releases

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-30b1d721b4600691c4d503cd19db1e85_r.jpg)

4. 点击Source code，下载netflix-1080p-1.21.zip压缩文件，并解压缩

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-9283e896012ffaafc33b79136070fa1d_r.jpg)

5. Chrome右上角，自定义及控制-更多工具-扩展程序

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-8654980f23b98e8b28b10c8f25f3cc6a_r.jpg)

6. 右上角，打开开发者模式

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-c1d45dd8e30687530588ffdc59d25eee_r.jpg)

7. 点击「加载已解压的扩展程序」，选择刚解压的文件夹

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-b2b4276ac481fa07197b669ca8e8a199_r.jpg)

8. Chrome右上角，扩展程序-更多操作-选项

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-cff98a7f00a74533a043c73bb513107f_r.jpg)

9. 前两行打勾之后Save

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-0ad2e7f841c46413fb1ad814c9713e09_r.jpg)

10. 清除「缓存的图片和文件」，之后重新加载Netflix网页，再播放影片画质就变成1080P了

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wdm1732418365/CDN/New%20folder/v2-2589e2fb8a7fb5c9ec97c7584c1c8628_r.jpg)
